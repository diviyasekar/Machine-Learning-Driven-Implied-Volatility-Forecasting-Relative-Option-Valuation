\section{Corporate Vision, Mission \& Core Values}

\subsection{Vision Statement}
To be the benchmark wealth management, investment consulting, and derivatives advisory institution in Tamil Nadu, recognized for ethical stewardship, technological agility, and superior risk-adjusted wealth creation across macroeconomic market cycles.

\subsection{Mission Statement}
To empower investors with bespoke, research-grounded financial solutions across mutual funds, equity cash and derivatives, fixed-income instruments, and risk-protection architectures. Surabhi Investment Services fulfills this mission by combining deep domain expertise, disciplined portfolio monitoring, transparent advisory frameworks, and state-of-the-art computational market analytics.

\subsection{Core Values}
The corporate ethos is anchored upon five institutional pillars:
\begin{enumerate}\setlength{\itemsep}{0pt}\setlength{\parskip}{0pt}
    \item \textbf{Fiduciary Integrity:} Uncompromising transparency in advisory, fee structures, and recommendation rationales, prioritizing the client's long-term financial security over short-term transaction incentives.
    \item \textbf{Quantitative \& Research Rigor:} Anchoring investment and derivatives guidance on verifiable empirical evidence, econometric principles, and mathematical risk modeling rather than speculative conjecture.
    \item \textbf{Client Centricity:} Tailoring multi-asset portfolios to the idiosyncratic risk tolerance, investment horizon, cash-flow liquidity requirements, and life goals of each investor.
    \item \textbf{Technological Agility:} Deploying cloud-native client portals, real-time analytics dashboards, and automated market monitoring pipelines to deliver institutional-grade transparency.
    \item \textbf{Regulatory Compliance:} Rigorous adherence to regulations established by the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI), Association of Mutual Funds in India (AMFI), and national exchanges (NSE/BSE).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Industry Problem Statement Faced by the Firm}
With the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) emerging as the global leader in equity derivatives volume, trading in NIFTY 50 weekly options surged among institutional and advisory clients. The firm's derivatives advisory desk encountered three critical operational bottlenecks:
\begin{enumerate}\setlength{\itemsep}{0pt}\setlength{\parskip}{0pt}
    \item \textbf{Naive Implied Volatility Assumptions:} Traders frequently assume short-term stationarity ($\widehat{\text{IV}}_{t+5} = \text{IV}_t$), failing to anticipate intraday volatility shocks and smile curvature shifts.
    \item \textbf{Manual Identification of Relative Mispricing:} Rapid quote revisions and wide bid-ask spreads make it difficult to systematically separate fundamentally rich from cheap options contracts in real time.
    \item \textbf{Extreme Expiry-Day Greek Instability:} As contracts approach weekly expiration ($\tau \to 0$), gamma surges and vega vanishes, creating non-linear regimes where standard pricing models experience structural breakdown.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Institutional Mandate \& Project Objectives}
To address these challenges, the leadership assigned the candidate to design, backtest, and operationalize an end-to-end quantitative analytics engine for NIFTY 50 weekly options:
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{0pt}\setlength{\parskip}{0pt}
    \item \textbf{Automated Market Data Harvesting:} Implement an asynchronous pipeline ingesting live 5-minute NIFTY 50 option chains via the Upstox API v2 with complete bid-ask and open interest depth.
    \item \textbf{Machine-Learning IV Forecasting:} Develop Random Forest and XGBoost models benchmarked against persistence under strict chronological splitting to predict 5-minute IV shifts ($\Delta\text{IV}_{t+5}$).
    \item \textbf{Relative Valuation Screener:} Formulate a quantitative screener that compares market IV against model-projected IV to isolate actionable rich and cheap contracts for spread execution.
    \item \textbf{Scenario Stress Testing Engine:} Construct a multi-factor simulation engine computing Greek exposures under simultaneous spot index and volatility shocks.
    \item \textbf{Trader Decision-Support Dashboard:} Deploy a production Streamlit web application providing real-time smile visualizations, model forecasts, and risk diagnostics for the desk.
\end{itemize}
